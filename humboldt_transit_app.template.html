Research prototype. Metrics come from deterministic event-based Python passenger simulations, precomputed for the displayed scenarios. Movement playback illustrates timetables and interpolated paths—not live C++ or a measured navigation model. Route distances, battery energy, demand, and flood outages are hypothetical. Ferndale is served by electric bus; no navigable Eel River hovercraft route is assumed. Real implementation requires hydrographic survey, tidal and weather modeling, dock studies, wildlife assessment, and demand validation.
